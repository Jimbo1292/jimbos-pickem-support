# Jimbo's Pick'Em Challenge Support

Need help with Jimbo's Pick'Em Challenge?

## Common topics

- Creating or joining a Pick'Em group
- Making and submitting weekly picks
- Group access or join-code issues
- Account login problems
- Account deletion
- Privacy questions
- Reporting inappropriate group or profile content

## Contact

Support email: **jpc.app.support@gmail.com**

Please include a brief description of the problem. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, group passcodes, or other sensitive credentials.

## Account deletion

Registered users can request permanent account deletion from within the app under **More -> Delete Account**.
