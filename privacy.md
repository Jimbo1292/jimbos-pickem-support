# Privacy Policy for Jimbo's Pick'Em Challenge

Effective date: **October 17, 2026**

Operator: **Jim Hand**

Privacy and support contact: **jpc.app.support@gmail.com**

This policy describes how Jimbo's Pick'Em Challenge (the “App”) handles
information when you create an account or use the App.

## Information we handle

We handle the following information to operate the App:

- Your email address and authentication account information, including an
  account identifier managed through Supabase Auth.
- Your profile information: display/profile name, selected preset avatar, and age
  division. Before creating an account, the App requests birth month and year
  only to confirm that you are at least 13. It does not request a day or full
  date of birth, and it does not retain the submitted month or year. The profile
  stores only the successful verification result, server timestamp, and method.
- Your private-group information: group names, memberships, administrative roles, join code,
  and passcode verification data. Group passcodes are stored as bcrypt hashes,
  not as readable passcodes.
- Your game activity: college-football picks, confidence values, submission
  status, standings, and historical competition results.
- Private safety and moderation actions: reports about fellow group members,
  including reason and optional details, and group-admin block records.
- If you choose to turn on push notifications, notification registration and
  delivery information: a random installation identifier generated by the App,
  the Expo push token assigned to that app installation, mobile platform, the
  signed-in account associated with the installation, active/inactive status,
  and notification event, delivery, ticket, receipt, error, and timestamp
  records. The App stores your per-account notification choice on your device.
  The server stores whether the matching installation is active; it does not
  receive a separate list of per-notification-type preferences.
- Basic technical and security information generated by Supabase and related
  infrastructure, such as IP address, user agent, request and authentication
  logs, and timestamps.

The current App does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, an analytics SDK,
a crash-reporting SDK, or collection of location, contacts, health information,
payment information, advertising IDs, photos, or audio. We do not sell personal
information.

## How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, authenticate users, provide
private groups, save and lock picks, calculate standings, enforce group and
pick-visibility rules, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and operate
and secure the App.

If you opt in to push notifications, we use the notification information to
send game-week reminders and results, open the relevant group and week when you
tap a notification, prevent duplicate or stale sends, and reconcile provider
delivery errors. Notification delivery is optional and is not used for
advertising, marketing, tracking, or analytics.

## Community protections

The App has no public social feed, chat, or media-posting feature. Profile and
group names are checked at the server-side write boundary for configured
objectionable terms. From **Groups → Members**, you may report a current fellow
group member using a listed reason and optional short description. Reports are
not visible to other members or the reported player and are available only for
authorized service handling.

Group admins may block an eligible non-admin member and later unblock them.
Blocking removes that player&apos;s access to the group and prevents rejoining until
an admin unblocks them; unblocking does not restore membership automatically.
Completed standings, scores, confidence results, and legitimate historical
display names remain intact.

Confidence points are game-scoring points used only for private-group
standings. They have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money or
anything of value.

The App has no real-money wagering, deposits, withdrawals, gambling, or cash
prizes.

## Service providers and disclosures

We use [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) to provide authentication,
database services, Edge Functions, and related infrastructure. Supabase
processes the account, profile, private-group, game, notification, and basic
technical/security information described above on our behalf.

When you opt in to push notifications, we use Expo's Push Service and Apple
Push Notification service (APNs) to deliver them. Expo receives the Expo push
token and the notification payload, passes the notification to APNs for an iOS
device, and provides ticket and receipt results. APNs processes a device token
and the payload to route the notification to your device. A payload can include
the notification title and message, a private-group name, and internal group,
week, and destination identifiers needed to open the correct App screen. We do
not intentionally include your email address, profile name, picks, confidence
values, or Supabase account identifier in the provider payload.

[Expo states](https://expo.dev/privacy-explained) that it stores end-user push
tokens and processes notification content only as long as needed to deliver it;
its [push documentation](https://docs.expo.dev/push-notifications/sending-notifications/)
states that receipts clear after 24 hours. APNs may retain an undelivered
notification until the event-specific expiration time supplied with the
notification. Expo and Apple also process information under their own privacy
terms.

The App's server-side service obtains public college-football schedule, ranking,
and scoreboard data from CollegeFootballData.com (CFBD). The implementation
does not send your email address, account identifier, profile, group,
selections, or device data to CFBD.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We
may disclose information where required by law or to protect the security,
rights, or operation of the App.

## Retention and account deletion

You can permanently delete your account in the App from **More → Delete
Account**. The App asks you to reauthenticate and confirm the deletion. When
the deletion completes, the authentication identity and user-owned raw App data
are deleted according to the implemented deletion flow.

To preserve legitimate completed competition standings in a private group that
remains active, we may retain a de-identified, frozen aggregate
completed-game result. It is displayed only as `Deleted player`. This archive
does not retain your account identifier, email address, profile, display name,
raw pick, or selected team. Deleted identities do not remain displayed as
personally identifying user profiles. We do not retain unfinished, live, or
simulation history in that archive.

If you owned a group with other members, the App transfers ownership under its
server-side group rule so the group can continue. If you were the sole member,
the group is removed.

Application data is retained while needed to provide the service or until it is
deleted according to the account-deletion flow. Supabase Pro project logs are
currently available to the operator for approximately 7 days, and Supabase Pro
daily database backups are currently available for approximately 7 days. We do
not promise that all provider-side operational or security data is deleted after
exactly seven days: Supabase may retain or process data according to its own
service, security, and legal obligations.

The local per-account notification choice, random installation identifier, last
Expo push token, and registration revision remain on the device until changed
or the App's local storage is cleared. Account deletion does not clear these
local values, but the deleted account no longer has a matching server record.

In the App database, installation and token rows remain while the associated
account exists, including after they become inactive. Notification event rows
remain until their associated account, group, or week is deleted. Ticket and
receipt rows remain until their associated event, token, or account is deleted.
A pending receipt lookup expires after 24 hours, but its database record is not
automatically deleted. The current implementation has no general automatic
notification-record cleanup job.

When an in-App Turn Off update succeeds, the installation and token are marked
inactive so the App will not select them for new sends; historical delivery and
receipt records are not immediately deleted. Account deletion removes the
account-linked server installation, token, notification event, and receipt
records through the implemented deletion flow. Provider-side data follows the
Expo and Apple handling described above and their applicable terms.

## Security

We use access controls, authentication, server-side authorization, and
transport security designed to protect App information. No security system is
perfect, so please use a unique password and keep your account credentials
private.

## Children and age considerations

The App is intended for users age 13 and older and is not directed to children
under 13. Account creation uses the transient birth-month/year check described
above and blocks users who cannot be confirmed as 13 or older. We do not
knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.
If Jim Hand learns that an under-13 child provided personal information, he will
take reasonable steps to delete it. Parents or guardians may contact
jpc.app.support@gmail.com.

## Your questions and choices

For privacy questions, safety concerns, or to exercise applicable privacy
rights, contact **jpc.app.support@gmail.com**. You can also request account
deletion in the App as described above.

Push notifications are off until you choose to turn them on and grant the
mobile operating system's permission. You can turn them off for the current
account and installation from **More → Help & Privacy → Push Notifications**.
When that update succeeds, the App deactivates the server registration. If it
fails, the App tells you that it could not update the device so you can retry.
You can also disable notification permission immediately in iOS Settings.
Turning notifications off does not affect your ability to use the rest of the
App. When you sign out, the App makes a best-effort attempt to deactivate the
installation's server registration before completing sign-out.

For app support and safety concerns, contact **jpc.app.support@gmail.com** or
use the public Support page linked from **More → Help & Privacy**.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the App change. We will post
the current version at the public Privacy Policy URL and update the effective
